# Security Policies and Procedures

## Reporting a Bug

Palo Alto Networks takes product security very seriously. If you believe you have found a vulnerability in one of our products, we ask that you follow responsible disclosure guidelines, contact us at [psirt@paloaltonetworks.com](mailto:psirt@paloaltonetworks.com), and work with us toward a quick resolution to protect our customers.

Refer to the [Palo Alto Networks Product Security Assurance and Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/product-security-assurance) for more details.
